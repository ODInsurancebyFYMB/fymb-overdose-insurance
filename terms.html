Insruance Rules

Insurance must be purchased BEFORE you take the drug of choice. Send the agreed number of Xanax to activate your policy. Clearly state which insurance policy you are purchasing. Must send FYMB the Overdose event log within 2 hours of purchasing the policy. You must overdose on the specific drug covered by your policy to qualify for a payout. Payouts are calculated based on the number of Xanax used to purchase your policy. No overdose = No payout. Your insurance premium is non-refundable. Be honest and provide proof of your overdose so your claim can be verified.

Insurance coverage

Single-use policies cover the stated overdose type and payout for the selected insurance units. Xanax payments cover Xanax, Happy Jump, Candy Jump, Ecstasy Overdose's as described in each policy.

7 Day Xanax Insurance

The fixed premium is 24 Xanax. The payout is 37 Xanax per qualifying Xanax overdose within the seven-day coverage period. Multiple qualifying Xanax overdoses are covered. Ecstasy overdoses are not covered.

FYMB LOTTO

FYMB LOTTO costs 20 Xanax and pays an additional 60 Xanax if a qualifying overdose occurs during the eligible single-use insurance. It is not available with 7 Day Xanax Insurance. The exact payment message is FYMB LOTTO.

Payment and claims

Send payments to FYMB [4318666] with the required policy message. Retain evidence of payment and overdose and contact FYMB to request a payout. The website does not automatically verify payments or issue payouts.

Final sales

All sales are final. FYMB LOTTO entries without the required message are not eligible for the bonus and are not refunded. No qualifying overdose means no FYMB LOTTO payout. Any Xanax sent without the correct policy stated in the message your payout will defualt to the lowest payout offered

These are the published website rules. Clarify any uncertainty with FYMB before sending payment.
